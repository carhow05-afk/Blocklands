▦ Blocklands

Explore a colourful 3D block world. Chop trees, dig underground, build a cabin or make anything you like.

Move with the left joystick. Look by dragging the right side of the screen. Aim the crosshair at blocks, then use Mine or Place. Tap the bottom bar to change blocks. Jump to climb hills.

Blocks are unlimited in this creative world. Your changes save automatically on this device.

Desktop: WASD, mouse drag to look, Space to jump, left click to mine, right click to place, keys 1–8 to select blocks.
